**1.1 Known gaps (each a bullet; include every one, reword only for grammar):**
- A full DPIA under Indonesia's PP 33/2026 is the next priority after submission.
- Gemini 3.5 Transcribe is in preview.
- Some models run on the `global` endpoint (today: transcription and labelling; see the table), so model processing
  may happen outside Jakarta even though services and stored data are in `asia-southeast2`.
- Capture needs the capture page open, the screen on and the phone plugged in.
- Consent is collected outside the app (the bee6–shop paper contract plus an emailed consent notice, privacy policy and
  terms). There is no in-app consent or revoked-consent state; revocation is handled manually, acknowledged and
  completed within 7 working days.
- A forgotten Mulai looks like a day off.
- Firestore TTL deletion lags (deletion is not instant).
- The pilot shop has not gone live yet; there is no pilot outcome. The closed loop is shown on the demo outlet, where
  "fast-forward to the review date" and each visit's re-opened debrief are simulated time.
- The suggested review verdict is plain code (a ±20% rule on per-day counts), not a model; supervisor and owner decide.
- The daily per-outlet token ceiling is not enforced yet; the demo has its own per-IP and daily caps.
- Constraints cannot be edited or deleted in the app; proposals respect them through the prompt and an
  acknowledgement, not a semantic check.
- The owner's approval screen has no cost/brand impact field.
- An outlet without a supervisor account cannot move proposals or reviews forward.
- The rubric and intervention library is a v0 written by the team, not yet reviewed by the domain expert.
- The on-device encrypted audio queue (P3) is not on; without it, about 10 minutes of speech survives a network drop.
